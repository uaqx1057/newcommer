Pre-Arrival Services

Strategic planning delivered before you land, so your first weeks in Canada start with clarity, structure, and momentum.

What We Cover

Plan Your Move Before You Land

Our pre-arrival programme gives you a practical settlement roadmap before departure. We work virtually with individuals and families worldwide to reduce uncertainty and shorten the adjustment period after landing.

  • Personalised settlement action plan and timeline
  • Housing research, neighbourhood guidance, and rental planning
  • Budgeting and financial preparation guidance
  • Banking setup, account options, and document readiness
  • Healthcare registration strategy and OHIP readiness
  • Job market overview and credential assessment
  • School system and family enrollment planning
  • Document checklist and pre-landing preparation

Key Outcomes

What You Leave With

A pre-arrival plan should move beyond generic advice and translate into decisions you can act on immediately.

Clear Settlement Roadmap

A structured plan covering housing, documents, finances, and next actions for the weeks before and after landing.

Virtual Planning Support

Remote guidance that works across time zones so preparation can begin well before travel and continue until departure.

Reduced Landing Friction

Better preparation means fewer avoidable delays around housing, banking, healthcare, and first-week logistics.

Who It Helps

Designed for individuals, couples, and families preparing to relocate.

Whether you are arriving through employment, study, sponsorship, or a broader migration plan, pre-arrival support helps you make better decisions before time pressure begins.

  • Clients comparing cities, neighbourhoods, and housing budgets
  • Families planning school enrollment and first-month logistics
  • Professionals organizing documentation, banking, and career preparation
  • Newcomers who want a structured start instead of reactive settlement
