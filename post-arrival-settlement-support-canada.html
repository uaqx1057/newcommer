Settlement Guide

Post-Arrival Settlement Support in Canada

Your first 90 days in Canada set the tone for everything that follows. This page focuses on the practical steps that reduce delays, confusion, and unnecessary stress after landing.

First 90 Days

Focus on the steps that create stability fastest.

Most newcomers need help prioritizing what to do first after arrival. The goal is to secure the basics quickly so housing, work, school, and family routines can start taking shape without avoidable setbacks.

  • SIN, health coverage, banking, and provincial document setup
  • Temporary and long-term housing decisions with rental-risk awareness
  • School enrollment, childcare planning, and neighborhood orientation
  • Resume, job-search, and professional transition support
  • Follow-through on day-one to day-90 settlement priorities
What Usually Matters First

A practical timeline for a stronger landing

Days 1-7

Immediate essentials such as address setup, phone, banking, SIN, and first-week logistics.

Weeks 2-4

Housing decisions, school enrollment, healthcare registration, and the start of local routines.

Months 2-3

Career momentum, credit building, transportation systems, and deeper local integration.
