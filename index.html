About Us

Established Expertise, Human-Centred Approach

At Newcomer Connect, we provide personalized immigration and settlement support to clients worldwide planning to move to Canada. Founded by Huma Tahir, a settlement support expert with 25+ years of experience, the team combines empathy, structure, and practical execution.

  • One-on-one guidance tailored to your goals and circumstances
  • Services delivered virtually worldwide through secure consultations
  • Physical support presence in GTA and Islamabad
  • Culturally sensitive, ethical, and transparent service model
  • End-to-end support from pre-arrival to full integration
