About Us

Established Expertise, Human-Centred Approach

At Newcomer Connect, we provide personalized immigration and settlement support to clients worldwide planning to move to Canada. Founded by Huma Tahir, a Canadian settlement expert with over 25 years of hands-on experience supporting newcomer families, the team combines empathy, structure, and practical execution.

  • One-on-one guidance tailored to your goals and circumstances
  • Services delivered virtually worldwide through secure consultations
  • Physical support presence in GTA and Islamabad
  • Culturally sensitive, ethical, and transparent service model
  • End-to-end support from pre-arrival to full integration

Founder & CEO

Meet Huma Tahir

Huma Tahir is a Canadian settlement expert and the Founder & CEO of Newcomer Connect, with over 25 years of hands-on experience supporting newcomer families.

As an immigrant herself, she understands that moving to a new country is not just a process, it is a life transition. Her approach focuses on preparing clients before they arrive and guiding them through the first 30 days in Canada, when the most important decisions are made.
