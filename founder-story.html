Founder & CEO

Meet Huma Tahir

Huma Tahir is a Canadian settlement expert and the Founder & CEO of Newcomer Connect, with over 25 years of hands-on experience supporting newcomer families.

She has worked directly with individuals and families navigating immigration, settlement, and long-term stability in Canada, guiding them through not just the paperwork, but the critical decisions that shape their future.

As an immigrant herself, Huma understands that moving to a new country is not just a process, it is a life transition. She understands the uncertainty, the pressure, and the responsibility of making the right choices for your family.

That is why her approach goes beyond traditional immigration support.

At Newcomer Connect, the focus is on preparing clients before they arrive and guiding them through the first 30 days in Canada, when the most important decisions are made.

What began as a mission to reduce confusion for newcomers has evolved into a structured, trusted support system, serving clients globally with virtual guidance and on-ground support in GTA and Islamabad.
