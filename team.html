Huma Tahir

Canadian settlement expertise shaped by lived immigrant experience and over 25 years of hands-on support for newcomer families.

Founder Story

Dedicated to Newcomer Success

Huma Tahir is a Canadian settlement expert and the Founder & CEO of Newcomer Connect, with over 25 years of hands-on experience supporting newcomer families.

As an immigrant herself, she understands that moving to a new country is not just a process, it is a life transition. Her leadership helps clients move through immigration, settlement, and long-term stability with practical guidance before arrival and through the first critical weeks in Canada.

Leadership Team

Meet Our Experts

Experienced professionals supporting your journey at every stage.

Jon Brock

Housing & Settlement Advisor

Jon Brock is a seasoned real estate professional focused on helping newcomers settle successfully in Canada. He supports individuals and families with renting, buying, and short-term accommodation options, including Airbnb, so they can make informed housing decisions during their transition.

Hussain Bukhari

Legal Counsel – Immigration & Refugee Law

Hussain Bukhari is a Canada-based immigration and refugee lawyer with extensive experience in refugee claims, humanitarian applications, and immigration appeals. As a former Member of the Refugee Protection Division, he brings deep insight into Canada's refugee determination system and legal framework.

Cherity Goerk

Mortgage & Home Financing Partner

Buying a home is one of the most important financial decisions you will make. With over 15 years of experience, Cherity helps simplify the mortgage process for newcomers by providing clear guidance and access to a wide range of lenders. She negotiates competitive rates and terms on your behalf, ensuring you get the best options available. Whether you are buying your first home or exploring better mortgage solutions, she makes the process smooth, transparent, and stress-free.

Abby Kayani

Director – Business Advisory & Investor Support

Abby leads international seminars and outreach focused on business migration and investment readiness. She supports investors and entrepreneurs with business planning, startup guidance, and understanding the Canadian market so they can make informed decisions.

Joshua-David Heath

Sales & Education Advisor

Joshua-David Heath guides newcomers on education pathways in Canada, including credential evaluation procedures, school/college/university systems, and student funding options. He helps families make confident education decisions before and after arrival.
