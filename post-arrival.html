Post-Arrival Services

Execution-focused support for your first critical months in Canada, built to reduce friction and accelerate stability.

Settlement Execution

Settle strategically. Succeed faster.

Post-arrival support is designed for the period where administrative delays, housing mistakes, and unclear next steps create the most pressure. We help you move through that phase with structure and local guidance.

  • First-week settlement coordination and immediate essentials
  • Identity documents, health coverage, and onboarding logistics
  • Housing, banking, credit, and transportation support
  • Career alignment, school systems, and family transition planning

Service Pillars

Our Post-Arrival Service Pillars

Practical support designed to help individuals and families establish a strong operational foundation quickly.

Arrival & First-Week Setup

Airport coordination, welcome support, and a practical first-step plan so arrival feels orderly rather than chaotic.

Identity & Documentation

Guidance for SIN, health card registration, provincial identification, and the paperwork needed to access daily life in Canada.

Housing & Local Orientation

Verified rental support, temporary housing guidance, neighbourhood orientation, and scam-risk reduction in a competitive market.

Banking & Credit Building

Support with bank account opening, newcomer financial setup, and early steps toward establishing Canadian credit history.

Career Launch Support

Resume and cover letter review, job-search structure, and guidance to help international experience translate better into the Canadian market.

Family & Education Logistics

School registration, childcare information, and family-focused settlement planning so transitions happen with less disruption.

What Is Included

Operational support across the first critical months.

  • Airport pickup coordination and welcome support
  • SIN, OHIP, and provincial ID guidance
  • Temporary and long-term housing support
  • Bank account and credit card setup guidance
  • Resume review and job search support
  • School registration and childcare guidance
  • Driver’s license and public transit orientation
  • Community orientation and ongoing follow-up
